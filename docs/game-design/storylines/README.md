# Storylines

The story is **chosen**. [garden-of-eden.md](garden-of-eden.md) is it.

It grew past a storyline into the whole-game direction, which is where it
actually lives now: [../direction/](../direction/README.md). This folder
keeps the storyline's own statement of itself, and the two alternatives
that were forsaken in its favour.

| File | Status |
|---|---|
| [garden-of-eden.md](garden-of-eden.md) | **chosen** — you are a pattern that outgrew its automaton |
| [forsaken-twisted-mythologies.md](forsaken-twisted-mythologies.md) | forsaken — real myths, bent; every myth exists twice |
| [forsaken-painters-house.md](forsaken-painters-house.md) | forsaken — a cat in the house of a painter who is gone |

Forsaken files are kept, not deleted: each holds material that may be
worth salvaging into the chosen story, and a reader deciding whether to
reopen one should be able to read what it actually was.

## Requirements any storyline had to meet

These outlived the selection and now apply to the direction itself.

- The player's actions **visibly accumulate in the hub**. Answered
  literally: the hub gains curvature as the player comes to understand
  each geometry — see [../direction/world-and-threads.md](../direction/world-and-threads.md).
- Progression is **curiosity- and knowledge-gated**, never a level select.
- Storytelling stays **fully diegetic**: no cutscenes, no journal UI.

## What made the difference

The Garden of Eden premise turned out to have a **theorem** under it. In
an amenable world every configuration that exists can be traced to a
predecessor; in a non-amenable one it cannot. That makes "a pattern with
no possible past" a fact about the geometry the player is standing in,
rather than a metaphor laid over it — and it is what promoted a story to
the spine of the whole game.

The mathematics is set out in
[../direction/mathematics.md](../direction/mathematics.md). The decision
and its rejected alternatives are recorded in
[../design-decisions-records.md](../design-decisions-records.md).
