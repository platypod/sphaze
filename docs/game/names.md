# The name

The game is called **UNBEGOTTEN**. It was prototyped under the name
*sphaze*, which named the prototype's one gimmick — a maze on a sphere —
and had to go: in this direction the sphere is the *starting cage*, the
thing the game is about leaving. A title naming the first hour is a title
that ages badly by hour three.

*Unbegotten* means: having no origin, not generated, not produced by
anything. It is the precise English word for the property the entire game
is about, and it happens to be the theological term for the same idea
(Nicene usage, of the Father) — which sits exactly alongside "Garden of
Eden" as the field's own borrowed vocabulary. That vocabulary is a
convenient fact rather than a reach: **"Garden of Eden", "orphan",
"quiescent", "ash" are the literature's actual technical terms**, so a
title in that register is faithful to the source material.

Why it won:

- **Exact.** It states the win condition and the theme in one word.
- **Ownable, though not virgin.** An obscure 2016 TV movie
  ([tt5660230](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt5660230/)) and a 1984 Lin
  Carter short story, *The Unbegotten Source*, share the word. **No
  games.** The games namespace is clear.
- **Right register.** Cold, old, a little sad. Matches a contemplative
  game about determinism and freedom, and matches the three endings.
- **It sounds like a title**, not like a word.

Known risks, kept rather than hidden: it reads religious to some, which
may misdirect; it is slightly archaic; a minority will hesitate over
pronunciation (un-be-GOT-en).

## Alternates considered, and why not

- **ORPHAN** — the literal technical term for a pattern with no
  predecessor; short and emotionally direct. Lost to a badly crowded
  namespace (a well-known horror film, several games).
- **AMENABLE** — the exact property that makes freedom *expensive*: a
  world that balances its books, where every exception is paid for by an
  erasure. Lost because as a title it reads soft and slightly corporate to
  anyone who does not already know the term.
- **NO PREDECESSOR** — stark and exactly the definition. Lost to sounding
  like a sci-fi thriller rather than a contemplative game.
- **SEVENFOLD** — from the `{7,3}` heptagrid. Lost for carrying the
  geometry half and none of the causation half, and a well-known band
  owns the search term.
- **STILL LIFE** — names the *failure* state (the ones who stopped), not
  the player; namespace also very crowded.
- **THE QUIESCENT** — names the dead background rather than the player.
- **ASH** — thematically apt, far too crowded.
- **THE FIFTH POSTULATE** — carries the geometry precisely, the automaton
  not at all.
